About

Untethered at 45

My parents died within seven months of each other, and they weren't the first people I loved to die. I sold the house I grew up in and left Fort Worth. I wrote the first draft of my first book in 72 days in Rio de Janeiro, then came back to the States and spent about a year as a FEMA disaster inspector and then an SBA loan officer. On Thanksgiving Day 2018 I flew to the Philippines on a one-way ticket that was meant to cover five or six weeks. I'm still here. Before all of that: financial services, life insurance, auto sales and retail.

In February 2024 I was on the back of a motorcycle (a habal habal, as the locals call it) when a small cargo truck hit us from behind. I don't remember it. I've only seen the security-camera footage. Five broken ribs, a broken right arm, three weeks in the hospital, and months of relearning simple things. I still write.

Abe Newmeyer is my pen name. I chose it for privacy, because I never planned to publish any of this.
